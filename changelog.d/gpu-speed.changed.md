- **Faster, with no pixel changed.** The GPU's shaders are compiled once per graphics device instead of each time the editor makes a bigger working area (a zoom to 100%, a portrait after landscapes, an export): on an Intel Arc 140V a rebuild at preview size drops from 20-30 ms to 3-5 ms on DirectX 12 and from 11 ms to 1 ms on Vulkan, and a second 16-bit TIFF export in a session no longer recompiles its shaders (330 ms to 60 ms). Every render tested is bit-identical (400 renders of five photos in every RAW rendering, on both backends). From RapidRAW `2c1dca20` (`src-tauri/src/gpu_processing.rs`).
- Preview JPEGs are encoded in parallel strips that decode to the same pixels: 64 to 31 ms at preview size, 778 to 120 ms at 24 MP, on 8 cores. This is the path for full-size previews and for the editor where the GPU viewer is off. From RapidRAW `35fdc7c9` (`src-tauri/src/preview_jpeg.rs`).
- Mask feathering runs on every core, byte-identical to before: 9-83 s to 0.3-2.9 s on a 24 MP mask at Feather 25-100. From RapidRAW `15a9ff2e` (`src-tauri/src/mask_generation.rs`, `gaussian_blur_mask`).
