- JPEG, TIFF and PNG photos with an embedded Adobe RGB, Display P3 or ProPhoto profile, and camera JPEGs marked Adobe RGB only in their EXIF, are shown in their own colours instead of washed out. A colour outside sRGB is clipped below zero, as a RAW's is, and kept above white for Highlights to bring back; HEIC photos are converted the same way, so their brightest P3 colours are no longer clipped at white. Plain sRGB and untagged files are unchanged, bit for bit, and edits saved before keep their look. Measured on five photos converted to each space with the profile embedded, CIEDE2000 against the sRGB original: as 16-bit TIFF, mean 0.34-3.06 (99th percentile 0.69-5.27) before, 0.01-0.02 (0.02-0.04) after; as 8-bit JPEG, ProPhoto 2.47-3.20 before and 0.76-0.96 after, Adobe RGB and Display P3 0.67-1.00 before and 0.68-0.85 after, where JPEG re-encoding is most of what remains. RapidRAW's pull of an out-of-sRGB colour toward its brightest channel landed further from the colour than clipping (mean 4.7 against 4.3 in Adobe RGB, 3.0 against 2.7 in Display P3, 10.3 against 9.5 in ProPhoto) and is not taken.
