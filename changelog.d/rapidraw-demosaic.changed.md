- **New edits are demosaiced the way RapidRAW 1.7.0 does it**: finer detail and less false colour, and far less on Fujifilm X-Trans. On Bayer sensors, green by RCD with the green checkerboard taken out, red and blue by residual interpolation (RI and MLRI), in place of rawler's PPG; on X-Trans sensors, Markesteijn's three-pass demosaic in place of rawler's simpler one. Measured on three EOS R6 Mark III photos binned to a true-colour picture and mosaiced again (CIEDE2000 mean, 99th percentile, CPSNR), today → now: Bayer 3.16 → 2.91, 10.90 → 9.82, +0.88 dB (street); 1.02 → 0.91, 4.17 → 3.42, +1.38 dB (beach); 3.27 → 3.04, 11.23 → 10.30, +0.80 dB (cyclist); X-Trans 4.03 → 3.02, 16.10 → 10.03, +3.27 dB; 1.53 → 0.95, 8.11 → 3.52, +5.39 dB; 4.08 → 3.19, 17.53 → 10.99, +2.88 dB. The green checkerboard in flat bright areas is four times weaker than PPG's. It runs on the CPU, the same picture on every machine: on a 32 MP photo the demosaic takes about 0.57 s for Bayer (PPG 0.08 s) and 1.6 s for X-Trans (rawler's 0.43 s), so a new edit opens about half a second later on Bayer. Pixels below black keep their colour as before (what the new demosaic clips at black is added back as a local mean, then the shadow rule judges it). Edits saved before keep rawler's demosaic bit for bit: a photo's decode rule gains a fourth value, `"pull-shadows-demosaic"`, the rule of photos with no edit yet. Cached thumbnails are made again once (pipeline 17).
